Regular Expression Summary

A regular expression quick reference organized by characters, character classes, anchors, quantifiers, and lookarounds.

lucaslzAuthor
May 29, 2022 3 min read
#Regular Expression
Content freshness notice Last verified on May 29, 2022. Versions may have changed; re-check the latest official docs before applying.
{ }

This page covers the regex metacharacters, character classes, and common pitfalls you’re most likely to encounter — handy for quick lookups and for double-checking your patterns as you write.

Characters

MetacharacterDescription
*Matching any character
|OR operator
\Escape

Whitespace Metacharacters

MetacharacterDescription
[\b]Backspace (deletes one character)
\fForm feed
\nNewline
\rCarriage return
\tTab
\vVertical tab

Character Classes

MetacharacterDescription
[]Matching a group of characters
[^]Excluding characters
[-]Character ranges
\d = [0-9]Any digit
\D = [^0-9]Any non-digit
\s=[\f\n\r\t\v]Any whitespace character
\S=[^\f\n\r\t\v]Any non-whitespace character
\w = [a-zA-Z0-9_]Any alphanumeric character or underscore _
\W = [^a-zA-Z0-9_]Any character that is neither alphanumeric nor an underscore _
\xHexadecimal: \x0A is character 10
\0Octal: \011 is character 9

Anchors

MetacharacterDescription
^Start of the string or line
$End of the string or line
\bWord boundary
\BNon-word boundary

Flags

MetacharacterDescription
iCase-insensitive
gGlobal
mMultiline

Backreferences and Lookarounds

MetacharacterDescription
()Subexpressions
(?:)Non-capturing group
\1Backreference matching matches the first capture group
(?=)Lookahead
(?!)Negative lookahead
(?<=)Lookbehind
(?<!)Negative lookbehind

Quantifiers

MetacharacterDescription
*Matches zero or more
*?Lazy version of *
+Matches one or more
+?Lazy version of +
?Matches zero or one (optional)
{n}Matches exactly n repetitions
{m,n}Matches at least m and at most n times
{n,}Matches at least n times
{n,}?Lazy version of {n,}
